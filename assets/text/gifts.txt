# What the Gift button on someone's card (its Pockets tab) can give them — see src/life/gifts.js.
#
# One gift a line: its emoji, its name, then after an @ anything special about it, then its {traits} as in
# people/loves.txt (see core/traits.js for every trait there is). Hovering over one in the Gift window lists what its
# traits do.
#
# [Keepsakes] go into their pockets (six at most) and stay there: their traits are on them for as long as they carry it.
# Taking one back out of their pockets (click it on the Pockets tab) takes its traits with it.
#
# [Consumables] are eaten or drunk straight away, leaving status effects (life/statuseffects.js EFFECTS) on them.
#
# After the @:
#   drunk:2           puts that status effect on them at that level (1 if left out); several allowed. With a snack,
#                     every mouthful (and one bought from a stall too)
#   3m                how long its status effects last (else each one's default)
#   coffee, beer, hotdog   put that snack from the stalls in their hand, to eat or drink as they go (its statuses
#                     come every mouthful)
#   cheer             lifts a sad or angry mood (😢, 😠, 🙄...) to a happier one from people/moods.txt, for good
#   wear=Sunglasses   they put it on (a style's name in the people model: Sunglasses, Glasses); anyone already wearing
#                     it starts out with one in their pockets, and taking the last one back out takes it off them
